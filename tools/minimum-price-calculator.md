# Minimum Price Calculator (FREE)

https://gettidycents.com/tools/minimum-price-calculator

Pricing from direct cost plus a markup quietly ignores the rent, the payroll and the weeks between delivering and getting paid. The free Minimum Price Calculator builds the price from the bottom up: direct cost per unit, its share of fixed costs at your real volume, the cost of waiting to be paid, then your target margin on the price. It also shows how the floor moves when volume changes.

Example: an item costs $18 to make, fixed costs are $6,000 a month and you sell 400. Each unit carries $15 of fixed costs, so break-even is $33. A 25% margin on the price needs $44, not the $41.25 a 25% markup gives.

FREE, no sign-up, runs in your browser.
