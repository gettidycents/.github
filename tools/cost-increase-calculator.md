# Cost Increase Calculator (FREE)

FREE Cost Increase Calculator: can your business absorb a price rise from a supplier?

A supplier writes that prices go up next month. The useful question is not the percentage but what it does to your own numbers. This free calculator applies the rise to the costs it really hits (direct costs lower your margin, fixed costs add to the monthly bill) and shows your new monthly profit, how long the cash above your reserve lasts, the price rise that wins it back and the largest rise you can take and still break even. Free, no sign-up, runs in your browser.
