# Break-Even Calculator (FREE)

https://gettidycents.com/tools/break-even-calculator

Break-even is the sales level where the business stops losing money. The free Break-Even Calculator turns your fixed monthly costs and your margin (or your price and unit cost) into break-even sales per month and per day, break-even units, and your margin of safety: how far sales can fall before you lose money.

Example: $9,600 of fixed costs a month, a $12 item that costs $4.80 to make. Each sale leaves $7.20, so you need 1,334 items, about $16,000 of sales a month. At $19,000 of sales you can lose about 15.8% before you stop making money.

FREE, no sign-up, runs in your browser.
