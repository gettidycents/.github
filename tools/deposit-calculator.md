# Deposit Calculator (FREE)

https://gettidycents.com/tools/deposit-calculator

A big order can be profitable and still drain your bank account before the customer pays. The free Deposit Calculator finds the smallest up-front deposit that keeps your cash above the reserve you set, and the deposit that covers all your own costs.

Example: a $30,000 order needs $18,000 of materials and labour up front. You have $20,000 in the bank and want to keep $8,000. Spare cash is $12,000, so the minimum deposit is $6,000 (20%). If you want the customer to fund the whole job, ask for 60%.

FREE, no sign-up, runs in your browser.
