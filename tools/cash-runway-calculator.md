# Cash Runway Calculator (FREE)

https://gettidycents.com/tools/cash-runway-calculator

A profitable business can still have a short runway: what matters is how long the cash above your safety reserve lasts if sales stop or fall. The free Cash Runway Calculator works from your gross margin, so a sales drop also removes the direct costs of the lost sales, and it measures runway both to your reserve and to an empty account.

Example: $40,000 of monthly sales at a 55% gross margin leaves $22,000; other outflows are $21,000, so cash still grows by $1,000 a month. But with no sales at all, $42,000 above the reserve lasts 2 months (about 60 days). A 20% sales drop turns the business into a $3,400-a-month loss.
