# Bank Reconciliation Difference Finder (FREE)

Find which entry made your bank reconciliation difference.

- Difference divisible by 9: two digits were probably swapped ($1,250 typed as $1,520 is out by $270 = 9 x 30).
- Half the difference equals an entry: it was posted on the wrong side.
- Difference equals an entry: it is missing from one side.

Take out deposits in transit and outstanding payments first. Integer cents, any currency, nothing stored, no sign-up.
