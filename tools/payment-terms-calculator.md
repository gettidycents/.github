# Payment Terms Cash Flow Calculator

**FREE** · FREE, no sign-up

**What does saying yes to Net 60 really cost you?**

When a customer asks for Net 60 instead of Net 30, the price stays the same but your cash does not. This free calculator shows the cash that stops arriving while the terms change, what that gap costs you a year, the same cost expressed as a discount on every sale, and the longest terms your cash can carry before it eats into your reserve.

**Example.** In the built-in example, moving part of the sales from Net 30 to Net 60 opens a one-off gap of $20,515. Financed at 12% a year, that costs $2,462 — the same as a 0.99% discount on every sale on those terms. The business could stretch to Net 85 before the gap reaches its safety reserve.
