# Tidy Cents

Tools for running your business: test a hire, a price, a big order or a loan against your own numbers before you commit.
No AI guesses: every result is calculated with fixed, tested formulas and shows its working. Any currency, 8 languages.

Website: https://gettidycents.com · Free tools, no sign-up: https://gettidycents.com/tools

## Free tools (no sign-up)

- [Cash Runway Calculator](https://gettidycents.com/tools/cash-runway-calculator): how many months your business can run on the cash it has.
- [Deal Safety Calculator](https://gettidycents.com/tools/deal-safety-calculator): lowest cash, minimum deposit and safe payment terms for a big order.
- [Minimum Price Calculator](https://gettidycents.com/tools/minimum-price-calculator): the lowest price you can charge without losing money.

## New this week

- [Contractor Bid & Job Control](https://gettidycents.com/product/contractor-bid-job-control): price a job from real quantities and costs and see the week your cash runs short before you sign.
- [Chargeback Evidence Desk](https://gettidycents.com/product/chargeback-evidence-desk): order records, delivery proof and messages in one file, with each dispute's deadline and what is missing.
