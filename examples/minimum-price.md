# The lowest price you can charge without losing money

A product costs **$12** to make. Fixed costs are **$6,000** a month over **1,000** units, so each unit carries **$6**.
The customer pays **60 days** later; waiting for $18 at **12%** a year costs `18 × 0.12 × 60 / 360 = $0.36`.

| Step | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Cost to make | $12.00 |
| Share of fixed costs | $6.00 |
| Cost of waiting to be paid | $0.36 |
| **Price floor** | **$18.36** |
| Price for a 25% margin: `18.36 / (1 − 0.25)` | **$24.48** |

The margin is divided out of the price, not added to the cost: `18.36 × 1.25 = $22.95` gives only a 20% margin.

All amounts are computed in integer cents.
