# A profitable job that runs your account below zero

A **$48,000** job: **$18,000** of materials in week 1, **$4,000** of labour every week for 5 weeks, a **30%** deposit (**$14,400**), the rest on completion. Profit on paper: `48,000 − 18,000 − 5 × 4,000 = $10,000`.

Start with **$15,000** in the bank:

| Week | Balance |
|---|---:|
| 1 | $7,400 |
| 2 | $3,400 |
| 3 | −$600 |
| 4 | −$4,600 |
| 5 | **−$8,600** (lowest) |
| Completion | $25,000 |

The deposit lifts every week by the same amount, so the smallest safe deposit is `14,400 + 8,600 = $23,000` (about 48% of the price). Staged payments are the other fix.
