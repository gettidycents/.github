# Bank Reconciliation Difference Finder (FREE)

https://gettidycents.com/tools/bank-reconciliation-difference-finder

A reconciliation difference is not random. Each kind of mistake leaves an arithmetic signature in the number: two swapped digits always move a balance by a multiple of 9, an amount posted on the wrong side moves it by twice that amount, and a decimal point one place out moves it by nine times the smaller amount. The free Bank Reconciliation Difference Finder reads that signature and points to the entry that explains it, with the proof.

Example: your adjusted records show $5,633.45 and the adjusted statement $4,913.45. The $720.00 difference is 9 × $80, and $1,198.75 keyed as $1,918.75 explains it exactly.
